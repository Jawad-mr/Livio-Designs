CASE STUDY BRAND IDENTITY & LOGO

Dexterity • Complete Identity System & Brand Guidelines

How Livio Designs built a cohesive visual architecture, geometric mark, and print collateral suite for an industrial innovation consultancy.

Client
Dexterity Systems
Discipline
Brand Design & Logo
Timeline
3 Weeks
Output
Full Identity Guidelines

The Challenge

Dexterity came to Livio Designs with a fragmented visual presence. Their collateral was inconsistently rendered across digital slide decks, print business cards, and social media channels. They required a unified identity system that signaled technical competence, geometric precision, and modern authority.

The Solution

Under JSN Creative direction, we established a strict grid-based mark derived from interconnected geometric nodes. We developed a high-contrast color palette anchored in deep cobalt and neutral canvas stone, paired with Plus Jakarta Sans for headers and Space Mono for technical callouts.

Project Impact & Deliverables

  • • Vector Primary & Secondary Monogram Marks (SVG, EPS, PDF, PNG)
  • • 36-Page Comprehensive Brand Guidelines Book
  • • Print-Ready Business Card Suite & Stationery Collateral
  • • Social Media Kit (Avatar templates, cover banners, post grid rules)
