Sweet Crumb BakerySweet Crumb BakerySweet Crumb BakerySweet Crumb Bakery

A full identity and point-of-sale system for a neighborhood bakery that needed to feel handmade without looking unfinished.

Client
Sweet Crumb Bakery
Scope
Identity + POS + Print
Timeline
6 weeks
Deliverables
Logo, packaging, system
Brief

The problem

Sweet Crumb was operating out of a single storefront with no consistent visual identity — different packaging for every product line, hand-written price tags, and a paper-only order system. The owner wanted something that felt warm and handmade, but professional enough to support catering and bulk orders.

Approach

What we built

We designed a wordmark built around a custom ligature in the "C" and "b," a warm two-color palette pulled from an actual proof of baked crust, and a packaging system that could be produced affordably at a local printer. Alongside the identity, we connected Livio's sister venture Nexus Agents to build a lightweight point-of-sale system so the brand extended into daily operations, not just the sign above the door.

Result

What changed.

01

Consistent shelf presence

Every product line now shares one packaging system instead of five mismatched ones.

02

Faster daily operations

Order-taking moved from paper tickets to a simple digital POS built alongside the identity.

03

Catering-ready branding

The identity now scales confidently to bulk and corporate orders, not just walk-in counter sales.
