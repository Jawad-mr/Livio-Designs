STUDIO

A design studio that treats every project like it'll be judged on the details.

Livio Designs is the design and branding arm of JSN CREATIVE — built on the belief that most local businesses deserve identity work that actually holds up, not a logo made in twenty minutes.

ORIGIN

Why "Livio."

Livio Designs grew out of the same instinct that built the rest of JSN CREATIVE — that small and mid-sized businesses are usually offered two bad options: a cheap, forgettable logo, or an agency retainer priced for a company ten times their size. Livio exists as the option in between: studio-level rigor, without studio-level overhead.

Every project runs through the same discipline as a real press proof — nothing ships until it's checked, aligned, and signed off. That's not a metaphor we use loosely. It's the actual standard.

LIVIO 099 — FOUNDING PRINCIPLE

"If it wouldn't survive a real press proof, it doesn't leave the studio."

WHAT WE HOLD TO

The values behind the work.

01

No templates, ever

Every identity starts from the business in front of us, not a pre-made kit with the colors swapped.

02

Systems over one-offs

A logo alone isn't a brand. We build the rules that let it work consistently everywhere it needs to appear.

03

Print discipline, digital speed

We hold digital work to the same precision print has always demanded — nothing "close enough."

04

Direct, not layered

You work with the people actually designing, not an account layer between you and the work.

PART OF

JSN CREATIVE is a multi-arm studio spanning education, freelancing, and technology. Livio Designs is its design and branding arm — working closely with sister ventures like Nexio Tech and Nexus Agents.
