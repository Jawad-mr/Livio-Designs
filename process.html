HOW WE WORK

From brief to press proof.

No mystery, no endless revision loop. Five stages, each with a clear deliverable, each signed off before we move to the next.

01

Discovery & brief

A conversation about your business, your audience, and what "done well" actually looks like for you — not a generic questionnaire.

LIVIO 014
02

Direction & concepts

We propose 2–3 genuinely distinct directions, each with rationale — never a single safe option dressed up as a choice.

PROCESS CYAN
03

Refinement

Once a direction is chosen, we push it further — tightening type, color, and system logic until it holds up under scrutiny.

PROCESS MAGENTA
04

Proof & sign-off

A full proof review before anything goes to production or launch — the same discipline a print house uses before running the press.

LIVIO 087
05

Delivery & handoff

Final files, guidelines, and source assets delivered in a format your team can actually use going forward.

TIMELINE

What to expect, roughly.

IDENTITY

2–4 weeks

Logo, palette, type system, and guidelines — from first call to final files.

WEBSITE

3–6 weeks

Depending on page count and whether it's paired with a new identity or built on an existing one.

FULL SYSTEM

6–10 weeks

Identity, web, and print together — the most thorough option, for a brand launching or relaunching in full.
