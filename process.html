METHODOLOGY

How Projects Run from Brief to Delivery

A systematic 4-step workflow that ensures design precision, eliminates revision endless loops, and delivers production-ready assets on schedule.

01

Discovery & Scope Brief

We start with a direct WhatsApp or brief consultation to establish project goals, target audience, reference benchmarks, deliverable specs, and deadline expectations.

02

Visual Strategy & Initial Concepts

We explore typographic pairings, grid layouts, color palettes, or initial video edit rough cuts. You receive 2–3 clear concept directions for review.

03

Refinement & Vector Execution

Upon selecting a concept direction, we refine vector paths, calibrate color profiles (RGB for screen, CMYK for press), polish sound/color in video edits, and perfect every detail.

04

Final Handover & Master Drive

You receive an organized master folder containing all production-ready files: vector SVGs, EPS, print PDFs, high-bitrate video MP4s, and open source editable assets.

STUDIO FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with Livio Designs under JSN Creative.

How fast is a typical project turnaround?

Standalone logo marks, poster designs, and single video edits typically complete in 3 to 5 business days. Full brand identity systems take 2 to 3 weeks.

What file formats do I receive at handover?

For graphic design & branding: AI, EPS, SVG, PDF, PNG, JPG. For video editing: 4K 10-bit MP4, MOV, ProRes, and project timelines if requested.

Why WhatsApp-first communication?

WhatsApp allows real-time video feedback, quick image approvals, and immediate updates without losing context in lengthy email threads.
